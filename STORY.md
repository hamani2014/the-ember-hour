# The Ember Hour — Story Bible

What happened to Ashen Vale, and how the player finds it out. All in-game text lives in `src/story.ts`.

## The truth (never told directly — pieced together from documents)

**Fifty years ago** — the Vance & Co. Ironworks ran day and night. Foreman R. Hale filed **eleven** pressure warnings about Furnace No. 2. The owner, **Edmund Vance**, returned every one unread: *the line does not stop*. At **3:17 AM** the furnace exploded and killed all **31 men of the night shift**. Vance called it "an act of God", destroyed the records, and shipped "31 crates of scrap" off the loading dock.

**The fire never went out.** It sank into the mine seams under the town and became a hunger — a **debt of thirty-one lives**, collected back **one hour at a time, always beginning at 3:17**. Clocks stop at 3:17. People lose hours, then names, then whole family members. The thing that collects it is the **Chronophage**: blind, made of soot, drawn to whoever carries the warmest light.

**Three years ago** — Mayor **Arthur Vance**, Edmund's son, found his father's ledgers. He ordered a **quiet evacuation** of the town (registry, page 42), then went to the Archive at 3:17 AM on October 14th to perform the old **Rite of Returning**. He never came back. Someone kept writing "14" on the calendar.

**The future** — the Chronos Institute studied the Vale as a place where time is *lost*, not passed. Expedition 9 found the source and the pattern: the rite may be finished or not, but **someone always arrives again — by car, at night, in the rain**.

**The rite** (Act IV) returns the stolen hour by burning what *is*, what *was*, and what *will be*, then the Keeper's Vow, before first light. If dawn finds it unfinished, the light doesn't take the debt — **it takes the keeper**.

## Julian Vance (left open on purpose)

Julian drives into the Vale with a three-year-old letter signed only **"A."** — *don't come looking for me*. The Mayor's unsent letter in his office says *"Some debts get handed down whether you want them or not."* The priest's note asks *"Who will it be next?"*. Whether Julian is Arthur's son, and whether he is the next keeper, is for the player to decide.

## The endings

- **Ending A — The Loop** (rite finished before dawn): the shadow shatters, morning, birdsong… a sharp pain, black, and Julian wakes on the asphalt beside his car as the engine dies. Again. *(The rite only decides who carries the light next.)*
- **Ending B — The Eclipse of Self** (dawn comes first): the sun turns the shadow to obsidian; outside, Julian sees himself arriving with a torch — and his hands bleed into darkness. He didn't drive it out. He became it.
